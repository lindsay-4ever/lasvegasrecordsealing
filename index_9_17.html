Common Questions

What People Ask Before They Start

How long does the record sealing process take?

From the time the Petition to Seal is filed to the receipt of a compliance letter from the FBI, the process usually takes six to eight months. In some cases, compliance has taken longer.

Are there records that can never be sealed?

If there are no convictions, all charges can be sealed. A Petition to Seal Records cannot be granted if you were convicted of: felony DUI, a felony sex offense, Home Invasion with the Use of a Deadly Weapon, Kidnapping of a Child, False Imprisonment of a Child, Sex Trafficking of a Child, or First Degree Murder with intent to commit Sexual Assault.
