Check Your Eligibility for Free

Answer a few questions about your Nevada case. It takes just a few minutes, and there's no cost or obligation.

Note: record sealing is different from a pardon — if you're not sure which applies to you, just start here and we'll point you in the right direction.

Step 1Nevada court check
Was your case handled in a Nevada state court?
This process only applies to Nevada state court records.
Which county was your case in?
Nevada's sealing law is statewide — this just helps us connect you with the right office.
Were you under 18 years old at the time of the offense?
What was the outcome of your case?
Choose the option that best describes your most serious or most recent case.
Was your case dismissed with prejudice, without prejudice, or are you not sure?
A dismissal "with prejudice" can't be refiled by the prosecutor — if you're not sure which kind, that's completely normal.
What type of offense was it?
If you're not sure, choose "Not sure" — we can pull your record and identify it.
Was this charge officially classified as a violent offense ("crime of violence") or burglary?
Was this your 1st, 2nd, or 3rd-or-more domestic battery conviction?
Was this your 1st, 2nd, or 3rd-or-more DUI, and did it involve injury or death to anyone?
Does your case involve any of the following?
Check any that apply. This doesn't automatically disqualify you — some cases have exceptions your attorney can review.
Have you fully completed your sentence?
This includes probation or parole, and paying off all fines, fees, and restitution.
When do you expect to complete it (approximately)?
A rough month and year is fine.
When did you complete your sentence?
Use your release date, or the date probation/parole ended or your last payment was made — whichever was last.
