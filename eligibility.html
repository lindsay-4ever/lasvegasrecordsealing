Step 1 Eligibility check
First — a few charges that cannot be sealed in Nevada.
Under Nevada law, a Petition to Seal Records will not be granted if you were convicted of any of the following:
  • Felony Driving Under the Influence (DUI)
  • A felony sex offense
  • Home invasion with the use of a deadly weapon
  • Kidnapping a child
  • False imprisonment of a child
  • Sex trafficking a child
  • First Degree Murder with intent to commit Sexual Assault
