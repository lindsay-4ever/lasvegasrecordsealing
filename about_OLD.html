Biography

Over Three Decades Defending Nevadans

Las Vegas Record Sealing is an independent legal marketing service. Sean P. Sullivan is our exclusive Las Vegas partner attorney — an independently licensed Nevada attorney who handles all legal services for clients referred through our platform.

Sean P. Sullivan has been defending Nevadans in the criminal justice system for over 30 years. His career in Nevada law began in 1994, and he has spent more than three decades developing deep expertise in Nevada criminal defense — including Nevada's record sealing statutes and procedures.

After serving as managing partner of Kelly & Sullivan, Ltd. from 1994 to 2010, Sullivan established his independent Las Vegas practice in 2011. He has handled thousands of criminal matters in the Nevada court system, including record sealing petitions across Clark County and throughout Nevada.

He is admitted to practice before the Nevada Supreme Court and the United States District Court for the District of Nevada, in addition to the Nevada State Bar.
