Your Privacy

Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect the information you share with us.

This policy explains how Las Vegas Record Sealing, operated by Stover Law (“we” or “us”), handles information on lasvegasrecordsealing.com. It applies to visitors from anywhere in the United States. We never sell your information, and we never share the details of your case with advertising or analytics companies.

1. What We Collect

  • What you tell us in our eligibility check — your name, phone, email, location, answers about your case, and any notes.
  • Browsing information collected automatically through cookies and pixels — such as your device, the pages you view, how you found us, and your general location.
  • Basic technical details when you submit our eligibility form, such as your IP address and browser type, used to block spam and fix problems.

2. How We Use It

To review your eligibility, contact you as you requested, let our attorneys handle your case, improve our website, and measure and show our ads.

3. Who We Share It With

  • Our attorneys, including our partner law firm.
  • Service providers that host our website, securely store information for us, and protect our form from spam. Cloudflare runs that spam check and sees technical details like your IP address, but never your answers.
  • Google and Meta, which receive browsing information only — never your name, contact details, or anything about your case.
  • Others when required by law.

4. Cookies, Tracking & Ads

We use Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, and the Meta Pixel to understand how our site is used and to measure our ads. Google and Meta may collect information about your activity over time and across other websites, and we may use it to show you our ads after you visit (retargeting). You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out, your Meta ad preferences, or the WebChoices tool, or by blocking cookies in your browser. Our site does not currently respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals.

5. Your Choices

Wherever you live, you can ask us to share, correct, or delete your information, stop contacting you, opt out of targeted ads, or not sell your information (we don’t). Email us at info@lasvegasrecordsealing.com — we’ll respond within the time required by law.

6. How Long We Keep It

We store your information securely and keep it until your record is sealed. If you’re not yet eligible, we keep it so we can let you know when you are, unless you ask us not to. If we review your case and find your record can’t be sealed, we delete your information after letting you know.

7. Changes & Contact

If we update this policy, we’ll post the new version here with a new effective date. Questions? Email info@lasvegasrecordsealing.com.

Effective date: October 29, 2026
