Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Las Vegas Record Sealing, operated by Stover Law (“we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you visit lasvegasrecordsealing.com or use our free eligibility check. It applies to visitors from anywhere in the United States.

The short version: We use what you tell us only to review your eligibility and contact you about record sealing. We never sell your information, and we never share the details of your case with advertising or analytics companies.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us

When you use our eligibility check, you may provide:

  • Contact details — your name, phone number, email address, and preferred way to be contacted.
  • Location — the county where your case was handled and the city, state, or country where you live now.
  • Information about your case — such as the court, the charge, whether you were convicted, and related dates.
  • Anything else you choose to share in the notes field or when you email us.
  • Your consent to be contacted, and the date and time you submitted the form.

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, we and our service providers may automatically collect information such as your browser and device type, the pages you view, the website that referred you, and your general location based on your IP address. We collect this using cookies, pixels, and similar technologies — see Cookies and Tracking below.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To review your answers and estimate whether you may be eligible to seal your record.
  • To contact you about your inquiry by phone, text, or email, as you requested.
  • To allow our attorneys to evaluate your situation, discuss your options, and, if you move forward, prepare and file your petition.
  • To operate, secure, and improve our website.
  • To understand how visitors find us and to measure the effectiveness of our advertising.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Submitting the eligibility form or sending us an email does not create an attorney-client relationship.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Our attorneys, including our partner law firm, so they can review your eligibility, contact you, and handle your petition.
  • Service providers that host our website and securely store information for us.
  • Analytics and advertising providers (Google and Meta), which receive information about your visit — such as the pages you view — and may use it to show you our ads on other websites and apps. We never send them your name, contact details, or anything about your case.
  • Others when required by law, or as part of a business transfer such as a merger or sale.

4. Cookies and Tracking

We use the following tools to understand how our website is used, to measure our advertising, and to show our ads to people who have visited our site:

  • Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager — to see how visitors use our site. You can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Meta Pixel — to measure the results of our Facebook and Instagram ads. You can manage how Meta uses this information in your Meta ad preferences.

Retargeting. After you visit our website, we may show you our ads on other websites and on social media such as Facebook and Instagram. These ads are based on the fact that you visited our site — not on anything you entered in our eligibility form.

Third-party tracking across websites. Google and Meta may collect information about your online activities over time and across different websites when you visit our site, and may use it to show you ads. You can limit this by blocking or deleting cookies in your browser, using the opt-out tools above, or visiting the Digital Advertising Alliance’s WebChoices tool.

Do Not Track. Some browsers send a “Do Not Track” signal. Because there is no common standard for these signals, our website does not currently respond to them.

5. Your Choices and Rights

Wherever you live in the United States, you may ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we have about you, and provide a copy;
  • Correct information that is inaccurate;
  • Delete your information, unless we are required to keep it;
  • Stop contacting you; or
  • Opt out of the use of your information for targeted advertising.

To make a request, email us at info@lasvegasrecordsealing.com. We may need to confirm your identity before responding, and we will respond within the time required by law. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

Requests not to sell your information. We do not sell personal information. If you would still like to make a request not to have your information sold, email us at the address above with the subject line “Do Not Sell.”

Some states give their residents additional privacy rights. We honor the requests described above for all visitors, no matter which state you live in.

6. How We Protect and Keep Your Information

We store your information in a secure database that is not publicly accessible, and we limit access to the people who need it to respond to your inquiry. No method of storing or sending information online is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please don’t send sensitive details about your case by email.

We keep your information until your record is sealed and your matter is closed. If you are not yet eligible, we keep your information so we can let you know when you become eligible, unless you ask us not to. If we determine that your record cannot be sealed, we delete your information after letting you know. We may keep information longer when required by law or by the professional rules that apply to attorneys. You can ask us to delete your information at any time by emailing us.

7. Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated policy on this page and change the effective date at the top. If we make material changes, we will also note them prominently on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to make a request, email us at info@lasvegasrecordsealing.com.
