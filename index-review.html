Common Questions

What People Ask Before They Start

What's the difference between record sealing and expungement?

Nevada doesn't have expungement — it has record sealing, which achieves a very similar result. A sealed record is hidden from public view and most background checks, giving you a clean slate for employment and housing.

Can I say I have no criminal record after sealing?

In most cases, yes. Nevada law allows you to answer "no" on job applications and housing forms that ask about criminal history, once your record is sealed.

Will my employer find out I'm sealing my record?

No. The process is confidential. Your employer is not notified, and once the record is sealed, it will not appear on standard background checks.

Are there records that can never be sealed?

Yes — felony sexual offenses, felony DUI cases, serious violent crimes, and crimes against children cannot be sealed under Nevada law. Most other offenses are eligible.
